## 1. Aesthetic Direction & Brand Identity

The video production for JusticeSphere AI must reject the visual cliches of generic corporate software explainers—such as flat cartoon vectors, generic 2D explainer animations, and synthetic robotic AI stock music. Instead, it must embody the visual language of a high-end, authoritative, sovereign legal technology platform: precise, intellectually rigorous, modern, and trustworthy.

The visual atmosphere is directly derived from the platform's actual design system implemented in `src/app/globals.css`:
- **The Modern Legal Parchment Palette:** Grounded in a dual theme system that balances timeless legal gravitas with bleeding-edge AI precision:
  - **Dark Base Canvas:** `#1D1D1B` (Deep Carbon Charcoal) provides depth, focus, and computational authority.
  - **Light Accent Parchment:** `#E7E7DD` (Warm Architectural Limestone) provides an organic, readable texture reminiscent of authentic legal documents.
  - **Sovereign Gold / Amber Accent:** `#D97706` / `#F59E0B` (Burnished Brass & Justice Gold) highlights active interactive elements, agent reasoning nodes, and verified legal citations.
  - **Risk Spectrum Tokens:**
    - High Risk / Statutory Breach: `#EF4444` (Vibrant Crimson Red)
    - Moderate Risk / Playbook Divergence: `#F59E0B` (Amber Warning)
    - Compliant / Verified Statute: `#10B981` (Emerald Green)
- **Typography Standards:**
  - Primary Western Headings & Body: `Inter` (Clean geometric grotesque; tracking -0.02em on headings).
  - Primary Bengali Legal Headings: `Tiro Bangla` (Distinguished formal Bengali serif with traditional proportions).
  - Code & Statutory Citations: `JetBrains Mono` or standard tabular monospace with subtle translucent badges.

## 2. Audio Engineering: Voiceover, Music & Sound Design

The sonic landscape of the video must establish narrative momentum, build tension during the problem reveal, provide crisp cognitive clarity during the product demonstration, and culminate in an inspiring resolution.

### Voiceover Direction & Pacing
- **Vocal Tone:** Deep, resonant, clear, warm, articulate English with a confident international cadence. Avoid hyperactive commercial announcer inflections; adopt the deliberate, credible pacing of a senior technology architect and legal advocate.
- **Delivery Speed:** Strictly calibrated to 120–128 words per minute (WPM). The 372-word script is calculated for exactly 180 seconds, ensuring generous 1.0 to 1.5-second pauses between major conceptual shifts to allow viewers to absorb complex visual screens.
- **Mastering Specifications:**
  - Peak Normalization: -1.0 dB True Peak
  - RMS Integrated Loudness: -16 LUFS (broadcast standard for digital platforms)
  - Equalization: High-pass filter at 80 Hz; gentle parametric boost (+2.0 dB) at 3.2 kHz for speech intelligibility; dynamic de-esser engaged between 5.5 kHz and 7 kHz.

### Musical Score Structure
The background music should be an original hybrid cinematic electronic composition structured into four distinct movements:
1. **Movement I: The Tension of Fine Print (0:00 – 0:40):** Sparse, atmospheric analog synthesizer drones with low cello undertones. A ticking analog percussion clock pattern emerges at 0:15, creating palpable anxiety around predatory contracts.
2. **Movement II: The Technological Awakening (0:40 – 1:25):** The clock dissolves into a warm, rhythmic arpeggiated synth line with organic piano chords, introducing optimism, precision, and architectural mastery as JusticeSphere AI is unveiled.
3. **Movement III: Computational Precision (1:25 – 2:30):** Driving minimalist electronic percussion (subtle bass kick and brushed hi-hats) enters during the ContractMind audit and architecture scenes, propelling the viewer through the fast technical execution.
4. **Movement IV: Sovereign Resolution (2:30 – 3:00):** Percussion pulls back, allowing a rich orchestral brass and synth crescendo to swell, resolving into an inspiring, sustained major chord that gently decays into total silence at exactly 3:00.

### Sound Effects (SFX) Palette
Every SFX must be understated and tactile, reinforcing the reality of software interactions:
- **Paper Rustle & Pen Hover (0:02):** Subtle foley sound of heavy bond paper sliding across a wooden desk.
- **Sub-Bass Braam / Hit (0:14 & 0:40):** Low-frequency cinematic impact (45 Hz) emphasizing problem urgency and product introduction.
- **Tactile Keystrokes (1:02):** Soft mechanical keyboard foley (Cherry MX Silent style) as the user enters the legal query.
- **Sovereign Shield Hum (1:07):** Ultra-short high-tech frequency sweep (1200 Hz to 2400 Hz) signaling client-side PII scrubbing.
- **Agent Stepping Chime (1:14):** Gentle crystalline tone as each `ThoughtLine` reasoning node illuminates.
- **Document Drop Thud (1:26):** Deep, satisfying acoustic thud as the PDF contract lands in the ContractMind dropzone.
- **Computational Scanning Sweep (1:30):** Delicate rising data-processing texture as the 14 clauses are parsed into the AST.
- **Statutory Stamp Seal (1:58):** Muffled physical wax-seal press sound as the certified advocate seal is applied.

## 4. Screen Recording Standards & Production Setup

To ensure that the recorded software interactions look indistinguishable from native desktop execution, production engineers must follow these technical guidelines:

1. **Resolution & Scaling:** Record strictly at native 4K (3840x2160) at 60.00 FPS. If 4K displays are unavailable, record at native 1080p (1920x1080) at 60 FPS with DPI scaling set strictly to 100% (Windows Display Settings -> Scale: 100%).
2. **Browser Preparation:**
   - Use Google Chrome or Brave in a dedicated guest profile with all extensions disabled.
   - Enter kiosk full-screen mode (Press `F11`) to completely hide the OS taskbar, window title bars, and browser URL omnibar.
   - Pre-warm backend endpoints by running one sample audit prior to recording so that LLM connections and Qdrant vector caches are fully hot.
3. **Mouse Movement Dynamics:**
   - Execute all cursor paths with continuous, deliberate acceleration and deceleration (ease-in-out).
   - Never perform rapid, nervous cursor shaking or erratic hovering. When moving between UI components, trace gentle linear or parabolic curves.
   - When clicking buttons, allow the cursor to rest on the element for 0.5 seconds before clicking, and remain stationary for 0.5 seconds following the click to let the user register the visual state change.
4. **Export Settings:**
   - Video Codec: Apple ProRes 422 HQ (master export) or H.264 / HEVC (MP4) at 50 Mbps constant bitrate.
   - Color Space: Rec. 709 with standard sRGB gamma (preventing washed-out dark theme contrast).
   - Audio Codec: Linear PCM 24-bit 48 kHz stereo.
